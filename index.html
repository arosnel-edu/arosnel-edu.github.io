Our Story

Rooted in
Sonoma County

FYRE Bartending was born from a love of this land and the incredible ingredients it produces. Based in Petaluma, we are dedicated to bringing a farm-to-glass philosophy to every event.

From small estate weddings in the hills of West Sonoma to corporate gatherings in the heart of San Francisco, we craft cocktail experiences that reflect the season, the setting, and the people we serve.

We pride ourselves by using local ingredients, fresh-pressed juices, house-made syrups, and foraged botanicals. Crafted with care, served with heart. We bring the best bar experience to you, ensuring an unforgettable celebration for you and your guests.
